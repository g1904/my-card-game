This session is all about how combat work.
- going first or second might be decided by event service for story reason.
- if not decided, then random pick who to go first.
- no mulligan mechanic, what player draws is the starting hand.
- the starting hand size is 4 (cards).
- the starting mana is max mana limit.
- 每个回合的步骤如下：
- 回合开始时，恢复满法力。
- 回合开始时，抓两张牌。
- 行动阶段，任意顺序出牌或使用法则/神通/古宝/法宝。
- 回合结束。
- each player gets 5 turns, and there are 10 turns total.
- Win condition to have higher 'momentum' after 10 turns.
- When drawing a card from a deck without any card left, lose 1 momentum for each card.
- set magic pack item limit to 9.
- remove creature / 灵宠 card type to simplify the game.
- and for 资源转换汇率与标杆，推迟到数值平衡阶段再定义，内容扩展先行。
